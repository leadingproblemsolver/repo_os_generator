# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate clearly, assume good intent, challenge ideas with evidence, respect user privacy, and preserve repository boundaries. Harassment, personal attacks, coercion, and misuse of customer data are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement should be resolved through decision records, tests, metrics, and proof artifacts rather than status, volume, or private context.
