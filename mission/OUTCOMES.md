# Outcomes

1. Customers receive grounded answers with explicit knowledge citations.
2. Returning customers experience continuity through consent-aware memory.
3. Human agents receive complete handoff context when escalation occurs.
4. Support operations gain auditable tickets, metrics, and improvement loops.
5. The system improves through evaluation without hiding failures.
